## 2024-07-18 - [Explicitly associating labels and making inputs and icon buttons accessible]
**Learning:** Always explicitly associate labels with inputs using React's `useId()` hook to avoid ID collisions in reusable components. When labels are visually hidden but necessary for context, add an `aria-label` attribute (e.g. `aria-label="Mint address"`) or `sr-only` class to the input for screen reader compatibility. Additionally, ensure icon-only buttons have an `aria-label` describing their action and apply clear `focus-visible` styles for better keyboard navigation.
**Action:** Consistently use `useId()` to generate unique IDs for dynamically rendered inputs and associate them with their respective `<label>` elements. Apply `aria-label` attributes to visually hidden inputs or icon-only buttons to convey their purpose to assistive technologies. Furthermore, add well-defined `focus-visible` states to enhance keyboard accessibility.
